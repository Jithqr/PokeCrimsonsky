CORE STRUCTURE (Read This First)
The Rift is an end-game dungeon system. It has:
12 Floors total
3 Chambers per Floor
2 Phases per Chamber: Phase 1 (First Half) and Phase 2 (Second Half)
Each Phase contains 1 Corrupted Boss Pokémon as the enemy
The player brings 12 Pokémon total into the entire floor — 6 assigned to Phase 1, 6 assigned to Phase 2
These 12 Pokémon are locked to that floor's 3 chambers. The same 12 Pokémon are used across all 3 chambers of the same floor. They do NOT reset between chambers.
HP and status carry over between chambers. If a Pokémon loses 300 HP in Chamber 1, it enters Chamber 2 with that same reduced HP. If a Pokémon faints, it stays fainted for the rest of that floor.
FLOOR DIVISION
Floors 1–6 (Resets Monthly)
Corrupted Boss Pokémon are non-legendary
Boss HP range: 1,000 – 1,500 HP
This section resets every month on the 1st. Players can re-attempt it after reset.
Floors 7–12 (Permanent Progression)
Corrupted Boss Pokémon are legendary
Boss HP range: 2,000 – 3,000 HP
This section does NOT reset. Once cleared, it stays cleared permanently on that account.

CHAMBER FLOW — STEP BY STEP
When the player enters a Chamber, this is the exact sequence:
Step 1 — Phase 1 Begins
Show the Phase 1 arena. Display the player's 6 Phase 1 Pokémon on one side.
Display the Corrupted Boss Pokémon on the other side with its full HP bar (1,000–1,500 for floors 1–6, 2,000–3,000 for floors 7–12).
The boss HP bar should be styled differently from normal — use a dark purple/corruption color with a pulsing glow animation.
Start the Phase 1 Timer (see Timer section below).
Combat plays out using the existing combat system.
Step 2 — Phase 1 End Conditions
Win condition: Boss HP reaches 0 before the timer expires.
Lose condition: Timer reaches 0 OR all 6 Phase 1 Pokémon faint.
If the player wins Phase 1, trigger the Phase Transition Animation (described below), then begin Phase 2.
If the player loses Phase 1, the chamber is failed. Show a "Chamber Failed" screen. The player may retry the chamber but their Pokémon HP does NOT reset — they retry with whatever HP they had when they entered this chamber attempt.
Step 3 — Phase 2 Begins
After the transition animation, show the Phase 2 arena.
Display the player's 6 Phase 2 Pokémon with their current HP (not full HP — they carry over from prior chambers).
Display a new Corrupted Boss Pokémon (different from Phase 1) with its full HP.
Start the Phase 2 Timer.
Combat plays out.
Step 4 — Phase 2 End Conditions
Same as Phase 1: win if boss reaches 0 HP before timer expires and before all 6 Pokémon faint.
If the player wins Phase 2, the Chamber is Cleared.
Show a Chamber Clear screen with the star rating for that chamber (see Stars section below).
After 2 seconds, transition to the next chamber or floor completion screen.
PHASE TRANSITION ANIMATION (Between Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the Same Chamber)

TIMER SYSTEM
Each Phase (Phase 1 and Phase 2) has its own independent countdown timer.
Timer values by floor group:
Floors 1–3: 3 minutes (180 seconds) per phase
Floors 4–6: 2 minutes 30 seconds (150 seconds) per phase
Floors 7–9: 3 minutes (180 seconds) per phase (longer due to higher boss HP)
Floors 10–12: 2 minutes 30 seconds (150 seconds) per phase
Timer display rules:
Show the timer as MM:SS in the top-center of the combat screen
Timer color: white by default
When 30 seconds remain: timer turns yellow and begins a subtle pulse animation
When 10 seconds remain: timer turns red and the pulse animation speeds up
When timer hits 0:00: freeze all combat, play a "time expired" flash (red screen flash), then show "Phase Failed — Time's Up"
Timer pauses:
The timer pauses during any dialogue, cutscene, or transition animation
The timer does NOT pause for move selection — players must act under pressure
STAR RATING SYSTEM (Per Chamber)
Each chamber awards 0 to 3 stars based on performance. Stars are calculated after BOTH phases of a chamber are complete.
Star conditions:
⭐ 1 Star — Both phases cleared. At least 1 Pokémon survived. No other conditions met.
⭐⭐ 2 Stars — Both phases cleared. At least 3 Pokémon across both teams are still conscious (not fainted) at the end of Phase 2. AND at least one phase was completed with more than 30 seconds remaining on its timer.
⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars — Both phases cleared. At least 4 Pokémon across both teams are still conscious. AND both phases were completed with more than 30 seconds remaining on their respective timers.
Important star rules:
Stars are calculated per chamber, not per phase
Stars are saved permanently — if a player earned 2 stars before and retries for 3 stars, only the highest star count is kept
The star count for a floor is the sum of stars from all 3 of its chambers (max 9 per floor)
Stars are displayed on the chamber card in the floor selection UI as filled/empty star icons

FLOOR LOCKED / UNLOCK LOGIC
Floor 1 is always unlocked
Each floor unlocks only when the previous floor has been cleared at least once (any star rating)
Locked floors display a lock icon over the chamber cards and a message: "Clear Floor [X-1] to unlock"
Floors 7–12 have an additional visual indicator (a legendary aura/glow on the floor card) to signal they contain legendary bosses
RETRY LOGIC
A player can retry any chamber at any time
When retrying, the Pokémon HP does NOT reset to full — they enter with whatever HP they had when they first entered the floor
The only way to get full HP on all Pokémon for a floor is to exit the floor entirely and re-enter (which triggers a new Team Assignment screen and resets all Pokémon HP to full for the new attempt)
Exiting mid-floor and re-entering counts as a fresh attempt — prior partial progress on chambers in that attempt is lost
